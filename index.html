About the project

TUDLO Cebuano Corpus in Mindanao

A digital research companion based on the undergraduate thesis proposal by John Harold M. Campado.

Purpose

The thesis proposes to develop a structured, accessible, and educationally grounded corpus of Cebuano/Bisaya texts from news articles and literary sources, supporting linguistic analysis and language documentation.

Why a corpus?

A searchable, organized collection of authentic texts can help researchers investigate vocabulary, register, frequency, concordance, and collocation patterns that are difficult to identify through casual observation alone.

Intended users

Language researchers, teachers, students, language planners, the Cebuano/Bisaya-speaking community, and future computational language technology developers.

Analytical tool

The thesis identifies AntConc as the primary corpus analysis instrument for frequency, concordance, and collocation analysis. This website’s lightweight analyzer is a supplementary utility only.

Study scope: The thesis primarily identifies news articles and literary texts, targets one million tokens, and limits its focus to Cebuano/Bisaya materials. Spoken-language-derived texts are discussed in the proposal, but the corpus composition and counts must be confirmed from the finalized dataset.
